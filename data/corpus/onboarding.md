# Northwind Labs Onboarding Guide

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to outline the standard onboarding process for all new employees joining Northwind Labs. We strive to provide a welcoming, structured, and efficient transition for every team member. This guide serves as a roadmap to ensure that you have the necessary tools, information, and support to succeed in your new role from your first day onward.

## 2. Orientation Program

Upon joining Northwind Labs, all new hires participate in a mandatory orientation program. This program is designed to introduce you to our company culture, internal processes, and organizational structure.

The orientation program lasts for 2 days. During these sessions, you will meet with various department heads, review our core values, and complete the necessary administrative setup. Attendance at all orientation sessions is required to ensure you are fully integrated into the team and understand the expectations of your position.

## 3. Equipment and Technology

We are committed to providing the hardware and software necessary to perform your job effectively. To ensure you are operational as soon as possible, your equipment will be provided according to the following schedule:

| Item | Provision Timeline |
| :--- | :--- |
| Laptop | Issued on day 1 |
| Peripheral accessories | Issued on day 1 |
| Security credentials | Issued on day 1 |

It is your responsibility to ensure that your laptop is kept in good working order and that you adhere to all company security protocols. If you encounter any technical issues with your issued equipment, please contact the IT support desk immediately.

## 4. Mentorship and Support

We believe that the best way to learn the ropes at Northwind Labs is through peer-to-peer support. To facilitate your integration, a buddy is assigned to every new hire.

Your buddy is an experienced team member who will act as your primary point of contact for day-to-day questions, office navigation, and informal guidance. While your manager remains responsible for your performance and professional development, your buddy is there to help you settle in, introduce you to colleagues, and provide context for our internal workflows. We encourage you to reach out to your buddy frequently during your first few weeks to ensure you have a clear understanding of your environment.

## 5. Probationary Period

All new employees are subject to a probationary period upon starting their employment. The purpose of this period is to allow both the employee and Northwind Labs to assess the fit for the role and to ensure that performance expectations are being met.

The duration of the probationary period is 6 months. Throughout this time, your manager will conduct regular check-ins to provide feedback on your progress, discuss any challenges you may be facing, and clarify goals. These check-ins are intended to be collaborative and supportive, focusing on your growth and your ability to contribute to the team’s objectives.

At the conclusion of the 6 months, a final review will be conducted to confirm the successful completion of your probation. If there are any concerns regarding performance or alignment with company standards, these will be addressed during your regular check-ins well before the end of this period.

## 6. Communication and Expectations

Northwind Labs values clear and transparent communication. As a new team member, you are expected to engage actively with your colleagues and participate in team meetings. We encourage you to ask questions, seek clarification when needed, and provide feedback on your onboarding experience.

Your success is our priority. By following the steps outlined in this guide, you will be well-positioned to contribute to our collective goals. We are pleased to have you with us and look forward to your contributions to Northwind Labs. If you require further clarification on any of the policies mentioned in this document, please consult with your manager or your assigned buddy.
