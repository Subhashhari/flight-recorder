# Northwind Labs IT Support Escalation Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define the standard procedures for escalating IT support requests at Northwind Labs. This document ensures that all technical issues are addressed with appropriate urgency and that resources are allocated based on the impact and severity of the reported problem. By establishing clear response expectations, Northwind Labs aims to maintain operational continuity and provide consistent service levels to all employees.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Northwind Labs personnel, including full-time employees, contractors, and temporary staff who utilize company IT infrastructure. It covers all reported technical incidents, service requests, and system outages managed by the IT Support department.

## 3. Incident Prioritization

To ensure that critical business functions remain operational, all incoming IT requests are categorized by priority level. Priority is determined by the scope of the impact and the urgency of the business requirement. The following table outlines the response time expectations for each priority level:

| Priority Level | Description | Response Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| P1 | Critical system failure or major business disruption | 15 minutes |
| P2 | Significant impact on productivity or departmental workflow | 4 hours |
| P3 | General inquiries, minor issues, or feature requests | 2 days |

## 4. Escalation Procedures

When an incident is reported, the IT Support team will perform an initial assessment to assign a priority level. If the issue does not receive a response within the designated response time, the ticket must be escalated to the next tier of technical support.

### 4.1 P1 Escalation
P1 incidents represent the highest level of urgency. If a P1 incident does not receive a response within 15 minutes, the on-call engineer must notify the IT Manager immediately. The IT Manager is responsible for mobilizing additional technical resources and providing status updates to relevant stakeholders until the issue is resolved.

### 4.2 P2 Escalation
P2 incidents involve significant disruptions that do not necessarily halt all business operations but require prompt attention. If a P2 incident has not received a response within 4 hours, the ticket will be automatically escalated to the Senior IT Support team. The Senior IT Support team will review the incident and determine if further escalation to specialized engineering teams is required.

### 4.3 P3 Escalation
P3 incidents are standard requests that have a lower impact on immediate business operations. If a P3 incident has not been addressed within 2 days, the ticket will be reviewed by the IT Support lead. The lead will assess the backlog and determine if the request requires additional resources or if it can be scheduled for a later time based on current workload priorities.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

Effective escalation requires clear communication and accountability across all levels of the IT department.

### 5.1 IT Support Staff
The primary responsibility of the IT Support staff is to triage incoming requests and attempt resolution at the first point of contact. If resolution is not possible, staff must ensure the ticket is accurately documented and escalated according to the timelines defined in this policy.

### 5.2 Senior IT Support and Engineering
Senior staff and engineers are responsible for handling escalated tickets that require deeper technical expertise. They are expected to provide clear updates on the progress of complex issues and collaborate with the IT Support staff to identify root causes and implement permanent solutions.

### 5.3 IT Management
IT Management is responsible for overseeing the escalation process and ensuring that the department meets the defined response time requirements. Management will review incident trends to identify areas where additional training or infrastructure improvements may be necessary to reduce the frequency of escalations.

## 6. Communication Standards

Communication is a critical component of the escalation process. Regardless of the priority level, the IT Support team must maintain professional and consistent communication with the user who submitted the request.

*   **Initial Acknowledgment:** Every ticket must be acknowledged within the defined response time.
*   **Status Updates:** Users must be provided with updates if the resolution time is expected to exceed the initial estimate.
*   **Closure:** Once a resolution is implemented, the IT Support team must confirm with the user that the issue is resolved before closing the ticket.

## 7. Policy Compliance

Adherence to this policy is mandatory for all members of the IT department. Failure to follow the escalation procedures may result in delayed resolutions and negative impacts on business productivity. The IT department will conduct periodic reviews of ticket data to ensure compliance with the response times and to refine the escalation process as the needs of Northwind Labs evolve.
