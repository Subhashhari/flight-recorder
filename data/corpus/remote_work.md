# Northwind Labs Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish clear guidelines for remote work at Northwind Labs. We recognize that flexibility is a key component of a modern, productive workplace. By providing a framework for remote work, we aim to support our employees in balancing their professional responsibilities with their personal lives while ensuring that our collective goals, collaboration, and operational standards remain high. This policy applies to all eligible employees and outlines the expectations for maintaining communication, availability, and performance when working outside of the office environment.

## 2. Eligibility and Scope

Remote work is a privilege designed to enhance productivity and employee well-being. Eligibility for remote work is determined by the nature of an employee's role and their ability to perform their duties effectively from a remote location. Employees must ensure that their remote workspace is conducive to professional work, including a reliable internet connection and a secure environment for handling company information. While we encourage flexibility, the needs of the business, team requirements, and individual performance will be primary factors in determining remote work arrangements.

## 3. Remote Work Schedule

To maintain a strong sense of team cohesion and ensure that we have sufficient in-person collaboration, Northwind Labs has established limits on the number of days employees may work from home. Employees are permitted to work from home for a maximum of 3 days per week. The remaining days must be spent in the office. This structure ensures that teams have regular opportunities to meet face-to-face, brainstorm, and participate in office-based culture.

## 4. Core Hours

Regardless of whether an employee is working from the office or remotely, it is essential that the team remains synchronized. To facilitate effective communication and collaborative meetings, Northwind Labs requires all employees to be available during our designated core hours. These core hours are 11:00-16:00 IST. During this window, employees are expected to be reachable via standard communication channels, such as email, instant messaging, and video conferencing tools. Meetings involving multiple team members should ideally be scheduled within this timeframe to ensure maximum attendance and participation.

## 5. Approval Process

All remote work arrangements must be reviewed and authorized to ensure alignment with team objectives and operational requirements. Employees wishing to establish a remote work schedule must seek approval from their line manager. The line manager is responsible for assessing whether the employee’s role is suitable for remote work and whether the proposed schedule supports the team’s ongoing projects. Managers may review these arrangements periodically and may adjust them if business needs change or if performance expectations are not being met.

## 6. Summary of Expectations

The following table outlines the key parameters of the Northwind Labs remote work framework to ensure clarity for all staff members.

| Category | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Maximum Work From Home Days | 3 days per week |
| Core Hours (IST) | 11:00-16:00 |
| Approval Authority | Line manager |

## 7. Performance and Communication

Remote work does not change the expectations regarding performance or output. Employees working remotely are expected to meet the same deadlines, quality standards, and performance goals as they would if they were working from the office. Communication is the cornerstone of a successful remote work policy. Employees are expected to be proactive in their communication, providing regular updates on their progress and flagging any potential blockers early. If an employee is unable to fulfill their duties due to technical issues or other unforeseen circumstances while working remotely, they must notify their line manager immediately.

## 8. Security and Confidentiality

Maintaining the security of Northwind Labs' data is a critical responsibility for every employee, regardless of their work location. When working remotely, employees must adhere to all company information security policies. This includes using secure, password-protected networks, keeping company devices updated, and ensuring that sensitive information is not accessible to unauthorized individuals. Any suspected security breach or loss of company equipment must be reported to the appropriate department without delay.

## 9. Professionalism and Conduct

While working remotely, employees are expected to maintain a professional demeanor. This includes being prepared for video meetings, dressing appropriately for virtual interactions, and ensuring that their remote environment is free from significant distractions. The core hours of 11:00-16:00 IST are intended to be a period of high focus and availability. Employees should manage their time effectively to ensure that they are fully engaged during these hours.

## 10. Policy Compliance

Adherence to this policy is mandatory for all employees participating in remote work. Failure to comply with the guidelines set forth in this document, including the core hours and the maximum number of remote days, may result in the revocation of remote work privileges. Northwind Labs reserves the right to modify this policy as necessary to align with evolving business requirements. Employees are encouraged to discuss any specific questions or concerns regarding their remote work arrangements with their line manager to ensure mutual understanding and success.
