# Northwind Labs Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to outline the leave entitlements for employees at Northwind Labs. We believe that a healthy balance between professional responsibilities and personal well-being is essential for maintaining a productive and engaged workforce. This document provides clear guidelines on the types of leave available, the accrual processes, and the administrative requirements for requesting time away from the office.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Labs. It is designed to ensure consistency, fairness, and transparency in how leave is managed across all departments. Employees are encouraged to plan their leave in advance to ensure that business operations continue smoothly during their absence.

## 3. Leave Categories
Northwind Labs provides three primary categories of leave to support employees in different life situations. These categories are defined as follows:

*   **Casual Leave:** Intended for short-term personal needs, errands, or brief periods of rest.
*   **Sick Leave:** Intended for recovery from illness, injury, or medical appointments.
*   **Earned Leave:** Intended for extended vacations, personal travel, or long-term rest.

## 4. Leave Entitlements
The following table summarizes the annual leave entitlements for eligible employees.

| Leave Type | Annual Entitlement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Casual Leave | 12 days |
| Sick Leave | 10 days |
| Earned Leave | 18 days |

## 5. Earned Leave Accrual and Carry Forward
Earned leave is accrued on a monthly basis to provide flexibility for employees throughout the year. Employees accrue earned leave at a rate of 1.5 days per month. 

To encourage employees to take regular time off for rest and rejuvenation, there is a limit on the amount of earned leave that can be carried forward into the next calendar year. The maximum amount of earned leave that an employee may carry forward is 30 days. Any earned leave balance exceeding this limit at the end of the calendar year will be forfeited unless otherwise approved by management under exceptional circumstances.

## 6. Sick Leave Requirements
Northwind Labs recognizes that health issues can be unpredictable. Sick leave is provided to ensure employees can prioritize their physical and mental health without financial worry. 

To maintain administrative integrity and ensure that medical needs are properly documented, Northwind Labs requires employees to provide a medical certificate for any sick leave taken after 3 consecutive days of absence. This certificate should be submitted to the Human Resources department upon the employee's return to work. Failure to provide the required documentation for extended sick leave may result in the absence being classified as unpaid leave or a deduction from other available leave balances.

## 7. Requesting Leave
All leave requests should be submitted through the internal company portal for approval by the employee’s direct supervisor. 

*   **Casual Leave:** Requests should be submitted as far in advance as possible to allow for team scheduling.
*   **Earned Leave:** Requests for extended periods of earned leave should be submitted at least two weeks in advance to allow for proper workload distribution.
*   **Sick Leave:** Employees should notify their supervisor as soon as it becomes clear that they are unable to attend work due to illness. 

While Northwind Labs strives to accommodate all leave requests, approval is subject to business requirements. Supervisors will review requests based on team coverage, project deadlines, and the nature of the employee's responsibilities.

## 8. General Provisions
Leave is a benefit provided to support the employee experience. Employees are expected to use their leave responsibly. Abuse of the leave policy, including the falsification of medical certificates or the misrepresentation of the reason for leave, may be subject to disciplinary action.

Employees who have questions regarding their specific leave balance or the application of this policy should consult their direct supervisor or the Human Resources department. Northwind Labs reserves the right to review and update this policy periodically to ensure it aligns with the evolving needs of the organization and its employees. We are committed to fostering a workplace culture that values the health and happiness of every team member.
