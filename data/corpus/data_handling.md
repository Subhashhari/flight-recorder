# Northwind Labs Data Handling Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a standardized framework for the classification, management, and retention of data within Northwind Labs. As our organization grows, it is imperative that all employees understand their responsibilities regarding the information they create, access, and store. This policy ensures that Northwind Labs maintains the integrity and security of its digital assets while complying with corporate governance standards. By adhering to these guidelines, we protect our intellectual property, client information, and internal communications from unauthorized access or accidental loss.

## 2. Data Classification Levels

To ensure appropriate security measures are applied to the correct information, Northwind Labs categorizes all data into 4 distinct levels. Every employee is responsible for identifying the classification of the data they handle and applying the corresponding security protocols.

| Classification Level | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| Public | Information intended for general release and external consumption. |
| Internal | Information intended for use by Northwind Labs employees only. |
| Confidential | Sensitive information that could cause harm if disclosed. |
| Restricted | Highly sensitive data requiring the highest level of protection. |

### 2.1 Public Data
Public data includes information that has been officially approved for release to the general public. Examples include marketing materials, published research, and public-facing website content. While this data does not require encryption, it must still be managed to ensure accuracy and consistency with the Northwind Labs brand.

### 2.2 Internal Data
Internal data consists of information that is intended for use by Northwind Labs employees but is not meant for public consumption. This includes general company announcements, internal newsletters, and non-sensitive project documentation. Employees should exercise caution when sharing this data to ensure it does not inadvertently reach external parties.

### 2.3 Confidential Data
Confidential data includes information that, if disclosed, could negatively impact the company’s competitive position or internal operations. This category includes internal strategy documents, non-public financial reports, and employee contact lists. Access to this data should be limited to those who require it to perform their job functions.

### 2.4 Restricted Data
Restricted data represents the most sensitive information held by Northwind Labs. This includes proprietary algorithms, trade secrets, and sensitive personal information regarding clients or staff. Due to the high risk associated with this data, additional security measures are mandatory. Access to Restricted data requires encryption at rest and manager approval.

## 3. Data Handling Requirements

The handling of data must align with its classification level. Employees are expected to utilize the approved tools provided by the IT department to store and transmit data. 

When handling Restricted data, employees must verify that the storage environment supports encryption at rest. If a system does not support this, the data must not be stored there. Furthermore, before accessing or processing Restricted data, the employee must obtain formal manager approval. This approval should be documented and kept on file for audit purposes.

For Internal and Confidential data, employees should utilize secure company-approved cloud storage and communication platforms. Avoid using personal email accounts or unauthorized third-party file-sharing services to transmit any company data.

## 4. Data Retention

Northwind Labs maintains a standard data retention policy to manage storage costs and minimize legal risk. Unless otherwise specified by a legal hold or a specific project requirement, the default retention period for all company data is 3 years.

### 4.1 Implementation of Retention
At the conclusion of the 3 year period, data should be reviewed for continued relevance. If the data is no longer required for business operations, it must be securely disposed of or archived according to the established IT procedures. 

### 4.2 Exceptions to Retention
There may be instances where specific regulatory requirements or legal obligations necessitate a longer retention period. In such cases, the department head must formally request an exception to the standard retention policy. This request must be reviewed and approved by the legal department. Once the exception expires, the data must be handled according to the default retention schedule.

## 5. Compliance and Enforcement

Compliance with this policy is mandatory for all Northwind Labs employees, contractors, and third-party partners. Failure to adhere to these guidelines may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. 

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams are aware of these policies and that the necessary approvals for Restricted data are obtained and documented. If an employee is unsure about the classification of a specific piece of data, they should consult with their manager or the IT security team before proceeding. 

By following these protocols, we ensure that Northwind Labs remains a secure and trustworthy environment for our employees and clients alike. We are all stewards of the data we handle, and our collective diligence is the primary defense against data loss and unauthorized disclosure.
