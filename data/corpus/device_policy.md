# Northwind Labs Device Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish clear guidelines for the acquisition, maintenance, and security of company-issued computing hardware at Northwind Labs. As our organization grows, it is essential that all employees understand their responsibilities regarding the equipment provided to them. This policy ensures that Northwind Labs maintains a secure, efficient, and standardized technological environment that supports our collective productivity while protecting our proprietary data and intellectual property.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff who are issued a company device. This includes, but is not limited to, laptops, tablets, and mobile workstations provided by Northwind Labs. By accepting a company-issued device, the user agrees to adhere to the security protocols and maintenance schedules outlined in this document.

## 3. Device Lifecycle and Refresh Cycle

Northwind Labs is committed to providing reliable hardware to ensure that all team members can perform their duties without technical hindrance. To maintain performance standards and ensure compatibility with current software updates, the company operates on a structured hardware refresh program.

All company-issued laptops are subject to a mandatory refresh cycle. This ensures that hardware does not become obsolete and that security vulnerabilities associated with aging equipment are minimized.

| Device Category | Refresh Interval |
| :--- | :--- |
| Standard Laptops | 36 months |
| High-Performance Workstations | 36 months |

Employees will be notified by the IT department when their device is approaching the end of its 36-month lifecycle. It is the responsibility of the employee to coordinate with IT to facilitate the data migration and exchange of the old hardware for a new unit. Failure to return the old device within the designated timeframe may result in the suspension of network access.

## 4. Security and Data Protection

The security of Northwind Labs’ data is a shared responsibility. Devices are the primary gateway to our internal systems, and their protection is paramount.

Employees must ensure that their devices are locked whenever they are left unattended. Passwords and multi-factor authentication must never be shared with others, including colleagues or family members. Furthermore, employees are prohibited from installing unauthorized software or modifying the hardware configuration of their devices, as these actions can introduce significant security risks to the corporate network.

## 5. Reporting Loss or Theft

In the event that a company-issued device is lost or stolen, immediate action is required to mitigate the risk of a data breach. The security of our information depends on the speed with which we can isolate a missing device from our systems.

If a device is lost or stolen, the employee must report the incident to the IT department within 24 hours. This reporting window is critical for the following reasons:

1. **Remote Wipe:** IT can initiate a remote wipe command to erase sensitive data from the device before it can be accessed by unauthorized parties.
2. **Access Revocation:** Credentials associated with the device can be invalidated to prevent unauthorized entry into company servers and cloud platforms.
3. **Insurance and Compliance:** Timely reporting is necessary for the company to fulfill its insurance obligations and regulatory reporting requirements.

Failure to report a lost or stolen device within 24 hours may be considered a violation of company policy and could result in disciplinary action. If the device is stolen, employees are also encouraged to file a report with local law enforcement and provide the IT department with the case number for our internal records.

## 6. Acceptable Use

Company devices are provided primarily for business purposes. While incidental personal use is permitted, it must not interfere with the employee’s work performance or violate any other company policies. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-issued hardware, as Northwind Labs reserves the right to monitor device activity to ensure compliance with security standards and legal requirements.

Prohibited activities include, but are not limited to:
* Accessing or distributing illegal content.
* Engaging in activities that could damage the reputation of Northwind Labs.
* Using company devices to facilitate unauthorized access to other systems.
* Disabling or bypassing security software, such as antivirus or endpoint detection tools.

## 7. Maintenance and Care

Employees are expected to handle company equipment with reasonable care. This includes keeping the device clean, avoiding exposure to liquids or extreme temperatures, and using appropriate protective cases during transit. 

If a device experiences technical issues or physical damage, it should be brought to the attention of the IT department as soon as possible. Employees should not attempt to repair the hardware themselves or take the device to a third-party repair shop, as this will void the manufacturer’s warranty and potentially compromise the security integrity of the machine.

## 8. Termination of Employment

Upon the termination of employment, for any reason, all company-issued devices must be returned to the IT department. This includes the laptop, power adapters, and any peripherals or accessories provided at the time of issuance. The return of equipment must be completed on or before the employee’s final day of work. Failure to return company property may result in legal action or the withholding of final compensation where permitted by law.

## 9. Compliance

Adherence to this policy is mandatory. Northwind Labs reserves the right to audit devices periodically to ensure compliance with these standards. Any employee found to be in violation of this policy may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. If you have questions regarding these guidelines, please consult with your manager or the IT department for clarification.
