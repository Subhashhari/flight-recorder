# Northwind Labs Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish clear guidelines for the reimbursement of business-related expenses incurred by employees of Northwind Labs. This document ensures that all expenditures are necessary, reasonable, and aligned with the operational requirements of the company. By adhering to these standards, Northwind Labs maintains fiscal responsibility while supporting the professional activities of its workforce.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Northwind Labs. It covers expenses related to meals, lodging, travel, and equipment procurement. All employees are expected to exercise sound judgment when incurring expenses on behalf of the company and to provide appropriate documentation for all claims.

## 3. Expense Categories and Limits
Northwind Labs categorizes employees into 5 levels (L1 through L5) based on their role and responsibilities. Each level is assigned specific daily or per-item spending limits for various expense categories. Employees are responsible for ensuring that their spending does not exceed the limits designated for their respective level.

The following table outlines the maximum reimbursement limits in INR per day for each category:

| Level | Meals | Lodging | Travel | Equipment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| L1 | 800 | 4000 | 3000 | 5000 |
| L2 | 1000 | 5000 | 4000 | 5000 |
| L3 | 1500 | 7000 | 6000 | 10000 |
| L4 | 2500 | 10000 | 9000 | 15000 |
| L5 | 4000 | 15000 | 15000 | 25000 |

## 4. Meal Expenses
Meal expenses are intended to cover the cost of food and beverages consumed during business travel or approved business meetings. Employees are expected to remain within the daily limit specified for their level. These limits are inclusive of taxes and gratuities. Expenses exceeding the daily limit will be considered personal and will not be reimbursed by Northwind Labs.

## 5. Lodging Expenses
Lodging expenses cover the cost of hotel accommodations during business travel. Employees should select standard, business-appropriate lodging that falls within their designated daily limit. If an employee chooses to stay in accommodation that exceeds their limit, they are responsible for paying the difference. Lodging costs should be booked in advance whenever possible to secure favorable rates.

## 6. Travel Expenses
Travel expenses include costs associated with transportation, such as train tickets, taxi fares, ride-sharing services, and public transit required for business purposes. Employees are encouraged to use the most cost-effective mode of transportation available. The daily travel limit applies to the total transportation costs incurred within a single day.

## 7. Equipment Procurement
The equipment category covers the purchase of necessary tools, software, or hardware required to perform job duties. Employees must obtain prior approval from their department head before making any equipment purchase. The limits provided represent the maximum amount allowed per item or per procurement request. Items exceeding these limits require additional justification and executive review.

## 8. Reimbursement Procedures
To receive reimbursement, employees must submit an expense report detailing all expenditures. Each entry must be accompanied by a valid receipt or proof of payment. Reports should be submitted in a timely manner following the conclusion of the business activity. Finance will review all submissions for compliance with the limits outlined in this policy. Any claims that do not meet the specified criteria or lack proper documentation may be rejected or returned for further clarification.

## 9. Compliance and Accountability
Northwind Labs maintains a policy of transparency and accountability. Employees are required to act in the best interest of the company when incurring expenses. Intentional misuse of company funds, submission of fraudulent claims, or consistent disregard for the established limits will be subject to disciplinary action. Managers are responsible for reviewing the expense reports of their direct reports to ensure adherence to these guidelines.

## 10. Policy Exceptions
In rare circumstances, business requirements may necessitate an exception to the established limits. Any request for an exception must be submitted in writing to the finance department prior to the expenditure. Exceptions are granted only on a case-by-case basis and must be supported by a clear business justification. Verbal approvals are not considered sufficient for reimbursement of expenses that exceed the defined limits.

## 11. Final Provisions
This policy is intended to provide a framework for professional conduct regarding company finances. By standardizing these limits, Northwind Labs aims to provide equitable treatment across all levels of the organization. Employees with questions regarding specific expenses or the application of these limits should consult with their manager or the finance department before incurring the expense. Northwind Labs reserves the right to update these guidelines as necessary to reflect changing business conditions.
