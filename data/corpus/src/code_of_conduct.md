# Northwind Labs Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Code of Conduct is to establish the standards of behavior expected from all employees, contractors, and representatives of Northwind Labs. Our reputation is built on integrity, transparency, and mutual respect. This document serves as a guide to ensure that our daily operations align with our core values and legal obligations. By adhering to these principles, we foster a workplace culture that encourages innovation while maintaining the highest ethical standards.

## 2. Workplace Conduct and Harassment
Northwind Labs is committed to providing a professional work environment free from discrimination and harassment. We believe that every individual deserves to be treated with dignity and respect. Harassment, whether verbal, physical, or visual, will not be tolerated. This includes conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive environment.

If an employee experiences or witnesses any form of harassment, they are required to report the incident to the Human Resources department. To ensure that concerns are addressed in a timely and effective manner, all reports of harassment must be submitted within 30 days of the incident. Northwind Labs maintains a strict non-retaliation policy for any individual who reports a concern in good faith.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest
Maintaining objectivity in our professional decisions is essential to the success of Northwind Labs. A conflict of interest occurs when an employee’s personal interests interfere, or appear to interfere, with the interests of the company. 

At Northwind Labs, conflict disclosure is mandatory. Employees must proactively disclose any situation that could potentially compromise their professional judgment. Examples of such situations include, but are not limited to, holding a financial interest in a competitor, engaging in outside employment that competes with company business, or having a personal relationship that influences business decisions. Failure to disclose a potential conflict may result in disciplinary action.

## 4. Gifts and Entertainment
While building professional relationships is encouraged, the exchange of gifts must be handled with caution to avoid the appearance of impropriety or bribery. Employees should never solicit gifts, and they must exercise sound judgment when accepting them.

To maintain transparency, Northwind Labs has established clear thresholds for gift acceptance. The following table outlines the policy regarding gifts and gratuities:

| Category | Policy Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Gift Acceptance | Permitted only if nominal in value |
| Gift Limit (INR) | 2000 |
| Reporting | Required for any gift exceeding the limit |
| Prohibited Items | Cash or cash equivalents |

Employees are strictly prohibited from accepting gifts that exceed the value of 2000 INR. If an employee is offered a gift that exceeds this limit, they must politely decline it or seek approval from their manager to donate the item to a charitable organization. Under no circumstances should an employee accept cash or cash equivalents, regardless of the amount.

## 5. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
Northwind Labs invests significant resources into the development of our proprietary technology and business strategies. Protecting this information is a collective responsibility. Employees are expected to safeguard confidential information, including trade secrets, client data, and internal communications.

Confidential information should only be shared with authorized personnel who have a legitimate business need to know. Employees must also respect the intellectual property rights of third parties. Unauthorized use of copyrighted materials or proprietary software is strictly prohibited and exposes the company to legal risk.

## 6. Use of Company Assets
Company assets, including computers, software, office equipment, and internet access, are provided to facilitate the performance of job duties. Employees are expected to use these resources responsibly and efficiently. While incidental personal use of company technology is permitted, it must not interfere with work productivity or violate company security policies.

Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-provided systems. Northwind Labs reserves the right to monitor the use of its assets to ensure compliance with security protocols and to protect the company from cyber threats.

## 7. Compliance with Laws
Northwind Labs operates in a complex regulatory environment. All employees are expected to comply with the laws, rules, and regulations of the jurisdictions in which we operate. This includes, but is not limited to, laws regarding anti-corruption, labor practices, and data privacy.

If an employee is unsure about the legality of a specific action or business practice, they should consult with their manager or the legal department before proceeding. Ignorance of the law is not an excuse for non-compliance.

## 8. Reporting Violations
The integrity of Northwind Labs depends on the vigilance of its employees. If you suspect that a violation of this Code of Conduct has occurred, you have a responsibility to report it. We encourage an "open door" policy where employees feel comfortable raising concerns with their direct supervisor or the Human Resources department.

All reports will be handled with the highest level of confidentiality possible. Northwind Labs will investigate all reported concerns thoroughly and take appropriate corrective action. We are committed to a culture of accountability, where ethical behavior is rewarded and misconduct is addressed promptly. By following these guidelines, every member of the Northwind Labs team contributes to a sustainable and successful organization.
