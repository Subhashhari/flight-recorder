# Northwind Labs Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Northwind Labs Travel Policy is to establish clear guidelines for business-related travel, ensuring that employees have the necessary resources to perform their duties while maintaining fiscal responsibility. This policy outlines the expectations for booking travel, managing expenses, and adhering to designated spending limits based on organizational levels. By following these procedures, Northwind Labs ensures consistency, transparency, and efficiency across all departments.

## 2. Booking Requirements

To ensure cost-effectiveness and availability, all travel arrangements must be made in advance. Employees are required to finalize their travel bookings at least 7 days prior to their departure date. Planning ahead allows the company to secure optimal rates and ensures that travel logistics are handled without last-minute complications.

## 3. Air Travel Guidelines

When booking air travel, employees are expected to prioritize economy class. For any flight duration that is below 6 hours, economy class is the mandatory standard. This policy is designed to balance employee comfort with the company’s commitment to sustainable financial practices. Employees should seek the most direct routes available when booking within these parameters.

## 4. Expense Limits

Northwind Labs categorizes employee spending based on organizational levels, referred to as L1 through L5. These categories dictate the maximum allowable daily expenditure for meals, lodging, travel, and equipment. The hotel cap is determined directly by the expense limits established for lodging in each respective level.

Employees are responsible for staying within these daily caps. Expenses incurred during travel must be documented and submitted for reimbursement in accordance with standard accounting procedures.

### Table: Daily Expense Limits (INR)

| Category | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 | L5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Meals | 800 | 1000 | 1500 | 2500 | 4000 |
| Lodging | 4000 | 5000 | 7000 | 10000 | 15000 |
| Travel | 3000 | 4000 | 6000 | 9000 | 15000 |
| Equipment | 5000 | 5000 | 10000 | 15000 | 25000 |

## 5. Lodging and Hotel Policy

Lodging expenses are governed by the limits provided in the expense table above. Employees must ensure that their hotel selection falls within the daily cap for their specific level. As noted, the hotel cap is derived directly from the expense limits. If an employee anticipates that their lodging costs may exceed these limits due to specific conference locations or high-demand periods, they must seek pre-approval from their manager before finalizing the booking.

## 6. Travel and Local Transportation

The travel category in the expense limits covers local transportation, such as taxis, ride-sharing services, and public transit, as well as incidental travel costs incurred while at the destination. Employees should utilize the most cost-effective mode of transportation that is appropriate for their business needs. Expenses exceeding the daily travel limit must be justified in the expense report.

## 7. Equipment Procurement

During business travel, employees may occasionally require additional equipment to fulfill their job responsibilities. The equipment category provides a budget for such needs, ranging from 5000 INR for L1 and L2 employees up to 25000 INR for L5 employees. This budget is intended for essential items required to maintain productivity while away from the office. All equipment purchased under this policy remains the property of Northwind Labs and must be accounted for upon the employee’s return.

## 8. Compliance and Reporting

Adherence to this travel policy is mandatory for all Northwind Labs personnel. Expenses that fall outside of the defined limits or that do not comply with the booking requirements may be subject to review or denial of reimbursement. Employees are expected to exercise good judgment and act in the best interest of the company when incurring travel-related costs.

All expense claims must be submitted with valid receipts for all items. By submitting an expense report, the employee certifies that the costs were incurred for legitimate business purposes and that they have adhered to the guidelines set forth in this document. If an employee is unsure about whether a specific expense is covered, they should consult with their manager or the finance department prior to making the purchase.

## 9. Policy Review

Northwind Labs reserves the right to review and update this policy as necessary to reflect changes in business operations or economic conditions. Employees will be notified of any significant changes to the expense limits or booking procedures. It is the responsibility of each employee to stay informed about the current travel policy and to ensure their travel plans align with these established standards.
