# Northwind Labs IT Security Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish the standards and requirements for information security at Northwind Labs. As our organization grows, the protection of our digital assets, intellectual property, and client data remains a top priority. This document outlines the mandatory security practices that all employees, contractors, and third-party associates must follow to maintain a secure working environment and mitigate risks associated with unauthorized access and cyber threats.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Northwind Labs personnel, including full-time employees, part-time staff, contractors, and consultants who have access to company systems, networks, or data. This policy covers all hardware, software, cloud services, and communication platforms owned or managed by Northwind Labs. Compliance with these security measures is a condition of employment and access to company resources.

## 3. Password and Authentication Standards

To protect company accounts from unauthorized access, Northwind Labs requires robust authentication protocols. All users must adhere to the following standards for account security:

*   **Password Complexity:** All passwords must meet a minimum length of 14 characters. Users are encouraged to use passphrases that are difficult to guess but easy to remember.
*   **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** The use of MFA is mandatory for all company systems, applications, and network access points. MFA provides an essential layer of security by requiring a second form of verification beyond a password. Users must configure their MFA settings upon account creation and maintain active status on all registered devices.

## 4. Device Security and Physical Access

Securing physical access to workstations is critical to preventing unauthorized data exposure. Employees are responsible for the security of their assigned hardware, whether working in the office or remotely.

*   **Screen Lock Policy:** To prevent unauthorized access to unattended workstations, all devices must be configured to lock automatically. The screen lock must activate after 5 minutes of inactivity. Users are encouraged to manually lock their screens (e.g., using keyboard shortcuts) whenever they step away from their desks, regardless of the duration.
*   **Physical Security:** Employees should ensure that sensitive documents are not left in plain view and that hardware is stored securely when not in use.

## 5. Incident Reporting and Phishing Awareness

Cybersecurity is a collective responsibility. Northwind Labs maintains a proactive stance against social engineering and phishing attempts. Employees must remain vigilant against suspicious emails, links, or requests for sensitive information.

*   **Phishing Reporting:** If an employee suspects they have received a phishing email or any other form of social engineering attempt, they must report it immediately. Reports must be submitted to the IT security team within 1 hour of the initial discovery. Prompt reporting allows the security team to neutralize threats before they impact the broader network.
*   **Reporting Channels:** Employees should use the designated internal reporting tool or email address provided by the IT department to submit these reports. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues; only send them to the security team for analysis.

## 6. Summary of Security Requirements

The following table summarizes the key security requirements established in this policy. All personnel are expected to be familiar with these figures and integrate them into their daily workflow.

| Security Control | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Minimum Password Length | 14 characters |
| Multi-Factor Authentication | Mandatory |
| Phishing Report Window | Within 1 hour |
| Automatic Screen Lock | After 5 minutes |

## 7. Compliance and Enforcement

Adherence to this IT Security Policy is mandatory. Northwind Labs reserves the right to monitor system activity to ensure compliance with these standards. Failure to comply with the requirements outlined in this document may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.

If an employee is unsure about how to implement these security settings or requires assistance, they should contact the IT support desk. Ignorance of the policy or the technical requirements does not exempt an individual from the responsibility to maintain secure practices.

## 8. Policy Review

This policy is subject to periodic review to ensure it remains aligned with the evolving threat landscape and the operational needs of Northwind Labs. Updates to these requirements will be communicated to all staff through official company channels. Employees are responsible for staying informed regarding any changes to security protocols and for updating their practices accordingly. By continuing to access Northwind Labs systems, employees acknowledge their agreement to abide by these security standards.
